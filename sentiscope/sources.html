Data & model sources

Non-commercial portfolio application. Default feedback is authored illustrative text, not actual customer records. No raw training comments are redistributed.

English sentiment: TweetEval (sentiment subset, CC BY 3.0, Barbieri et al. 2020 and Rosenthal et al. 2017).

itsonga sentiment: AfriSenti official release (CC BY 4.0 on official repository; Hugging Face mirror has conflicting noncommercial metadata, so this portfolio remains noncommercial). Xitsonga subset collected in Mozambique, not validated on South African customer feedback.

English emotions: GoEmotions (Demszky et al. 2020, Apache 2.0). Multilabel classifiers trained on the provided splits.

Optional browser Transformers: DistilBERT multilingual sentiment and multilingual MiniLM embeddings. First use downloads model files; performance varies by language and device. Language is user-selected, not inferred. Mixed sentiment and topic labels are explicitly heuristic. Not intended for consequential automated decisions.
